RUNE DUEL

Cards of the Four Winds

A card duel of ember, tide, grove and gale.
Summon creatures, cast runes, and break the Bramble Witch before she breaks you.

How to Play

  • Goal: reduce Sova the Bramble Witch from 24 life to 0. If yours hits 0, you fall.
  • Energy: you gain 1 energy gem each turn (max 8). Cards cost gems, shown top-left.
  • Playing: click a card in your hand to summon or cast it. Boards hold up to 5 creatures.
  • Attacking: click one of your ready creatures, then click an enemy creature — or Sova herself. A creature cannot attack the turn it is summoned, unless it has Rush.
  • Guard creatures must be attacked first. Spark burns a target when summoned. Mend heals you. Draw fetches more cards.
  • Kindred: a creature gets +1 attack while another creature of the same wind stands on your board.
  • Press End Turn when you are done, and watch what Sova answers with.
